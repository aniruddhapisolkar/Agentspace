## F1 — Helix, projection circle and pitch

+ Target heading: "Space curves"
+ Insertion anchor: after "The parameter $t$ is the angle of the projection, and $2\pi b$ is the vertical rise after one complete turn, called the pitch."
+ Mathematical claim: for $\mathbf{r}(t)=\langle a\cos t,a\sin t,bt\rangle$ with $a>0,$ $b>0,$ the projection onto the $xy$-plane is the circle $x^2+y^2=a^2$ and one turn rises by $2\pi b.$
+ Objects: the helix over $0\le t\le4\pi$; its vertical projection onto the $xy$-plane; the $z$-axis; a vertical segment of length $2\pi b$ joining two successive turns at the same angular position; the point at $t=0$ labelled $(a,0,0).$
+ Labels: $x,$ $y,$ $z,$ $a,$ $2\pi b,$ $\mathbf{r}(t).$
+ View: three-quarter view with the $z$-axis vertical. Draw the projection circle in a lighter stroke in the plane $z=0,$ with short vertical dashed lines from two points of the helix to their projections.
+ Distinction: schematic, not to scale; the helix should make two full turns so that the pitch is visible as the gap between successive turns.
+ Shading: none; line drawing with the helix in a strong stroke and the projection dashed.
+ Alt text: "A two-turn helix above its circular projection, with the vertical rise of one turn marked as 2 pi b."

## F2 — Secant vectors approaching the tangent

+ Target heading: "Derivatives and tangent vectors"
+ Insertion anchor: after "Geometrically, $\mathbf{r}'(t)$ is tangent to the curve at $\mathbf{r}(t)$ and points in the direction of increasing $t.$"
+ Mathematical claim: the difference quotient $[\mathbf{r}(t+h)-\mathbf{r}(t)]/h$ is a secant direction; as $h\to0$ it tends to $\mathbf{r}'(t),$ the tangent direction, provided $\mathbf{r}'(t)\neq\mathbf{0}.$
+ Objects: a smooth plane curve through $P=\mathbf{r}(t);$ the point $Q=\mathbf{r}(t+h)$ nearby; the secant vector from $P$ to $Q;$ the tangent line at $P$ with direction $\mathbf{r}'(t);$ a small arrow at $P$ labelled $\mathbf{r}'(t).$
+ Labels: $P,$ $Q,$ $\mathbf{r}(t),$ $\mathbf{r}(t+h),$ $\mathbf{r}(t+h)-\mathbf{r}(t),$ $\mathbf{r}'(t).$
+ Orientation: mark increasing $t$ with an arrow along the curve. Show two secants for decreasing $h$ to indicate the limiting direction.
+ Distinction: schematic; the curve should be visibly curved so that secant and tangent are distinct.
+ Shading: light shading under the curve is unnecessary; use one accent colour for the tangent.
+ Alt text: "A secant vector between two points of a curve approaching the tangent vector as the points coalesce."

+ Mathematical claim: one turn of the helix has length $2\pi\sqrt{a^2+b^2},$ the hypotenuse of a right triangle with horizontal leg $2\pi a$ and vertical leg $2\pi b.$
+ Objects: on the left, one turn of the helix with its projection circle; on the right, the same turn cut open and laid flat as a straight segment; a right triangle beneath it with legs $2\pi a$ and $2\pi b$ and hypotenuse labelled $L.$
+ Labels: $2\pi a,$ $2\pi b,$ $L,$ and the angle $\alpha$ with $\tan\alpha=b/a.$
+ Construction: a curved arrow from the helix to the unrolled segment indicates the cutting and flattening. Keep the correspondence between one full revolution and the horizontal leg explicit.
+ Distinction: schematic. The unrolled segment should be exactly the hypotenuse, not a separate arbitrary line.
+ Shading: light fill in the triangle; no 3D shading.
+ Alt text: "One turn of a helix unrolled into a straight segment that forms the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs 2 pi a and 2 pi b."
